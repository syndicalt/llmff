# llmff Blog Series QA Checklist

Use this after drafting each article directory.

## Required Files

- `article.md`
- `posts.md`
- `image-brief.md` when the article includes an image placeholder or when the
  series plan lists an image idea

## Article Checks

- Uses the title and thesis from `blog/series-plan.md`.
- Opens on a concrete tension, not a generic industry setup.
- Includes at least one manifest, command, schema, trace fragment, or concrete
  execution sequence.
- Includes one earned founder-flare line grounded by nearby mechanics.
- States `llmff` as a bounded execution runner/subprocess boundary, not an
  agent framework, serving layer, memory layer, scheduler, or PM layer.
- Keeps tradeoffs visible.
- Uses image placeholder text in the article when an image is expected:
  `[IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: ...]`.

## Companion Post Checks

- Contains 3-6 standalone X posts.
- Each post makes one point.
- Posts avoid launch-copy tone.
- At least one post is mechanism-first.

## Anti-Trope Checks

Search and remove unless intentionally quoted as a banned example:

- "In today's rapidly evolving landscape"
- "Let's dive in"
- "At its core"
- "This changes everything"
- "The result?"
- "Here's the thing"
- "not just"
- "unlock"
- "seamless"
- "robust"
- "game-changing"
- "revolutionary"
- "agents are dead"

## Final Read

- Delete throat-clearing.
- Replace vague value claims with mechanisms.
- Remove sentences that could appear unchanged in a generic AI startup essay.
- Read aloud and cut anything too polished to sound spoken by a real person.
